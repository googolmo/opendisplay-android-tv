# Store listing copy — English (United States)

**App name**

OpenDisplay TV Receiver

**Short description**

Turn your Android TV into a wireless display for your Mac.

**Full description**

Use your Android TV or Google TV as a wireless display for a Mac with OpenDisplay.

OpenDisplay TV Receiver listens for a connection from the OpenDisplay macOS app on your local network, then displays the incoming H.264 video on your TV. Use it to mirror or extend your Mac screen on a larger display.

Features:
- Discovers the receiver on your local network with Bonjour / mDNS.
- Decodes H.264 video with Android hardware acceleration.
- Choose Auto, 1080p, 1440p, or 4K UHD resolution modes.
- Shows a cursor overlay and a playback HUD with frame rate, bandwidth, and latency.
- Designed for Android TV and controlled with a TV remote.

Getting started:
1. Install OpenDisplay on an Android TV or Google TV device running Android 10 or later.
2. Connect the TV and Mac to the same local network.
3. Open this app, then connect to the receiver from the OpenDisplay app on your Mac.

This app is a receiver. The OpenDisplay macOS sender is required to stream a Mac screen. Streaming quality depends on your devices and network. A 4K mode is available, but smooth playback at that resolution depends on device decoder support and network capacity.

OpenDisplay is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with Apple or Google.
